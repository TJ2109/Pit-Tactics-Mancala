PIT TACTICS

A game of sowing, capture, and counting seeds. Play the old Mancala way, against an AI that never cheats.

How to Play

  1. Pick a pit on your side. Click any pit that has seeds in it.
  2. Sow counter-clockwise. One seed is dropped in each pit going right, skipping the opponent's store.
  3. Land in your store then go again. If your last seed lands in your own store, you get an extra turn.
  4. Land in an empty pit of yours then capture. You take that seed plus everything in the opposite pit into your store.
  5. Empty your side to end the game. Once one side has no seeds left, all remaining seeds go to their owner's store. Most seeds in your store wins.
